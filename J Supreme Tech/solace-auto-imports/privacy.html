Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Who we are

Solace Auto Imports Limited is a certified used car dealer based in Westmoreland, Jamaica. This policy explains what information we collect through this website (solaceautoimportsltd.com) and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, request vehicle sourcing, or ask about a listing, we collect the details you provide — your name, phone number, email address, and the content of your request. We only collect what you choose to submit.

How we use it

Your details go to our back office so we can respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, arrange viewings, and progress sourcing or import requests. We do not sell your personal information, and we won't add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

Where it lives

Enquiries are stored securely with our hosting and database providers, and alert emails are delivered to our inbox by our email provider. Access is limited to the Solace team.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookie-free page analytics to understand site traffic. It does not identify you personally or track you across other websites.

Your choices

Want your details corrected or removed? Call (876) 456-6976 or email solaceimports@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
