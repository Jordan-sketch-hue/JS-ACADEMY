About Solace Auto Imports Limited

Certified used car dealer based in Westmoreland, Jamaica. We import, sell and source quality vehicles for our customers.

Our Story

Solace Auto Imports Limited was founded with one goal — to make buying a quality used vehicle simple and stress-free for Jamaicans. From trusted overseas partners to local clearance and delivery, we handle the journey from showroom to your driveway.

Whether you're a first-time buyer or upgrading your daily ride, our team takes the time to understand what matters to you — budget, body type, fuel preference, and beyond.

Our Mission
