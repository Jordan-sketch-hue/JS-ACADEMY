Install it like an app — no App Store needed

When customers add the site to their home screen, it opens full-screen without browser chrome — indistinguishable from a native app.

  1. On iPhone, open https://solace-auto-imports.vercel.app in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an up-arrow)
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add — the Solace icon appears on the home screen
  5. Tap the icon — opens full-screen, no browser bars, just the app
Why this matters: a true native iOS app costs $99/year (Apple Developer Program) plus 2–4 weeks of build time and app review. "Add to Home Screen" gives 90% of the experience today, for zero extra cost. When sales justify it, the native app can come later.
