AXIOM · WHAT DO YOU WANT TO MAKE?

AXIOM will detect what you need — ad creative, AI image, stock media, reel, campaign — and route you there automatically.

OR PICK A MODE BELOW

🌐

WEBSITES & APPS

Generate full websites, landing pages, Next.js apps, WhatsApp bots, e-commerce stores — any web project from a single description.

→ Type your brief in the console above and hit RUN

🖼️

GENERATE IMAGES

Create AI-generated images using Flux. Describe what you want — logo backgrounds, product shots, lifestyle photos — then download or use in an ad.

→ Opens the IMAGES tab

🎬

BROWSE PHOTOS & VIDEO

Search millions of stock photos and video clips from Pexels and Pixabay. Preview, download, or drop them into any ad creative or website.

→ Opens PHOTOS & VIDEO tab

🎨

BUILD AD CREATIVES

Design sized ad creatives for Instagram, Facebook, YouTube — 1:1, 9:16, 16:9. Add text layers, B-roll backgrounds, AI captions, and export as PNG.

→ Opens AD CREATIVE tab

✏️

EDIT A SITE VISUALLY

After generating a site, open it in the visual editor. Click any element — headline, button, section — to edit text, change colors, or reposition it. No code needed.

→ Opens EDIT SITE tab (run a build first)

🎞️

COLOR GRADE IMAGES

Apply cinematic color grading to any image — 12 presets (Cinematic, Golden Hour, Noir, Cold Steel…) or manual exposure, contrast, saturation, vignette sliders. Export graded PNG.

→ Opens COLOR GRADE tab
